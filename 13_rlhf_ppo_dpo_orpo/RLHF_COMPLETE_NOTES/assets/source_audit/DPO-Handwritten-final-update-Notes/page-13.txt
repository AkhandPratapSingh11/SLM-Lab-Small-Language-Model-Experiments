DPO Goal: Chosen score > Rejected score

DPO compares how much the policy model promotes the chosen response and the rejected response relative to a

frozen reference model, and trains the policy so that the chosen response receives the higher relative likelihood.

DPO does not need exact reward numbers. It only needs to know whether the chosen response is better than the

rejected response.

DPO compares the Policy Model with the Reference Model. This comparison works like a hidden reward signal.

Therefore, DPO can train the Language Model directly without using a separate Reward Model, Critic Model, or PPO

training loop.

Implicit reward = hidden reward signal derived from the Policy Model’s probability relative to the Reference Model.
