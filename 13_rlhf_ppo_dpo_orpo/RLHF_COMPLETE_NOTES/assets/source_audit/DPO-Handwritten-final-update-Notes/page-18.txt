Overall Experimental Findings

With virtually no hyperparameter tuning, the original DPO paper found that DPO:

+ Performed similarly to or better than PPO-based RLHF across the tested preference-learning tasks.

+ Achieved a better reward-KL trade-off than PPO in controlled sentiment generation. In this experiment, DPO's

reward-KL frontier also outperformed PPO-GT, which had access to the ground-truth reward.

+ Outperformed PPO on TL;DR summarization, achieving an approximately 61% win rate, compared with PPOs best

result of approximately 57%. DPO was also more robust to changes in sampling temperature.

+ Showed strong performance on single-turn dialogue. It was the only computationally efficient method in the

experiment that improved over the preferred responses in the Anthropic HH dataset and achieved performance

similar to or better than the computationally expensive Best-of-128 baseline.

+ Was evaluated on language models with up to 6 billion parameters.
