Pretraining means predict the next token

Pretrained models are good at:

+ Generating fluent text

In Pretraining Model learns:

+ Patterns in data

* Grammar & language structure

+ General knowledge

But the training data may contain:

* Incorrect or misleading information

+ Not following instructions properly

* Not Giving consistently correct or helpful answers

“Pretraining makes the model knowledgeable, but not controllable and trustworthy”
