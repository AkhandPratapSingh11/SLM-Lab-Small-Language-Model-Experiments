Supervised Fine-Tuning teaches the model:

+ How to follow instructions

+» The expected format of answers

SFT teaches:

* “This is a good answer"

But it does NOT explicitly say:

+ "This answer is bad, avoid it”

What SFT does not guarantee

SFT does not explicitly teach:

+ Which answers are better than others

+ Which answers are unacceptable

+ Human values like politeness, safety, tone, or helpfulness

So during SFT:

+ Probability of good answers increases

+ Probability of bad answers can also increase accidentally
