Why do we need Preference Alignment?

Problem with only SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning)

Because Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) improves task performance

But it does not ensure that the model's behavior aligns with human values, safety, and expectations.

Alignment techniques like RLHF, DPO, and ORPO ensure that LLMs not only generate correct outputs

but also produce responses that are safe, helpful, and aligned with human preferences.

Preference alignment (also called preference learning) is now a critical part of modern LLM training—

it is no longer optional.

Modern LLMs are used in real-world systems such as:

+ Healthcare assistants

+ Finance tools

+ Education platforms

+ Enterprise copilots

In these environments, behavior matters just as much as correctness.
