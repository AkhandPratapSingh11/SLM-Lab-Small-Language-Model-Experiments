What is RLHF?

RLHF is a training method or process that uses reinforcement learning algorithms like PPO, along with human

feedback, to align language models(LLM) with human preferences.

SFT teach LLMs: “This is the correct answer”

RLHF teach LLMs: “This answer is better than the other one”

Since in the real world: There can be multiple correct answers.

Prompt: Explain Al

Output A — simple and clear:

Al means machines that can learn, think, or make decisions like humans. For example, chatbots, self-driving cars,

and recommendation systems use Al.

Output B — complex and confusing:

Al is a computational paradigm involving algorithmic approximations of cognitive processes through statistical

inference, representation learning, and optimization over high-dimensional data distributions.

Both can be technically correct.

but Human prefers A

What is Reward Modeling?

Reward Modeling is the process of training a model to evaluate, score, or rank responses according to human

preferences.

In RLHF: Reward Model = A proxy for human judgment

Why is Reward Modeling Needed?

An LLM does not naturally know:

+ what is “better”

+ what is "safe"

+ what is "clear"

The Reward Model acts as a proxy for human feedback. It learns human preferences from chosen/rejected examples

and then approximates how a human would score a response.
