DPO compares how much the policy model promotes the chosen response and the rejected response relative to a

frozen reference model, and trains the policy so that the chosen response receives the higher relative likelihood.

Reward values themselves are not unique; only the reward difference between chosen and rejected responses

matters. DPO represents reward as Blog

(n_O/nt_"ref" ). Therefore, the Language Model's relative probabilities act

as an implicit Reward Model. This allows DPO to optimize preferences directly without a separate Reward Model,

Critic, or PPO training loop.
